The Fallen Kingdom

Once, Elyndor was the greatest kingdom in the world.
Its people discovered how to harness the Arcane and built an empire unlike anything humanity had ever seen. Cities floated above the clouds, weapons were forged from pure magic, and ancient crystals powered machines capable of bending reality.
But their ambition became their downfall.
The rulers of Elyndor attempted to control The Origin—the source from which all Arcane power was believed to come.
They failed.
In a single night, the kingdom disappeared.
The survivors called it The Great Silence.
For centuries, the ruins remained untouched.
Until now.
